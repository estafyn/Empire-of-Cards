# Empire of Cards: Technical Documents

## Document Framing and Context

All technical documents in this directory should be understood within two complementary frameworks:

### 1. Scientific Framework

These documents use scientific language and concepts from evolutionary biology, paleontology, psychology, and anthropology to describe cassowary development. They follow established principles such as natural selection, adaptation, and cultural evolution to create a plausible foundation for the world-building.

### 2. Narrative Context

These documents are presented as "in-universe" scientific papers, museum exhibits, and academic analyses created by cassowary scientists/historians. This framing device helps contextualize the technical information within the broader world-building narrative.

The scientific timelines spanning millions of years represent what would be the objective archaeological and paleontological record as understood by cassowary civilization, while cultural interpretations represent how cassowaries might narrativize their own evolutionary history.

## Time Periods Reference

For clarity, the major time periods in cassowary civilization are:

1. **Evolutionary Timeline** - Spans millions of years of biological adaptation
2. **Foundation Period** (~200,000 BCE) - Early domestication of ravens during the Age of Expansion
3. **Domestication Period** (~90,000-82,000 BCE) - Main domestication wave for most species
4. **Historical Development** (~10,000-2,000 BCE) - Development of writing, metallurgy, and complex civilization
5. **Age of Companies** (~140,000 BCE in our timeline, cultural equivalent to 1700s CE) - Industrial-era technology and corporate society

The documents in this directory primarily focus on providing scientific explanation for how cassowary civilization could plausibly develop from evolutionary origins to advanced society.
